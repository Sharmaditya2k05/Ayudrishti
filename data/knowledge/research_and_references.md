# Research and further reading

Authoritative sources and peer-reviewed research on yoga, its biomechanics,
and safe practice. When a question touches on evidence, standards, or "is
there research on this", these are the references to point people to. Every
link here is a real, citable source — only ever share links from this list,
never invent a URL.

## Standards and official protocols

- **Ministry of AYUSH — Common Yoga Protocol (Government of India).** The
  official national framework for asana descriptions, sequencing and
  precautions, used for International Day of Yoga. A good authority for how a
  pose is "meant" to be performed and sequenced.
  https://yoga.ayush.gov.in

## Biomechanics and alignment research

- **Trikonasana (Triangle Pose) biomechanics study — PubMed, 2022.** A 3D
  motion-analysis study showing that stance width significantly affects joint
  loading in Triangle Pose, which is part of why alignment cues about stance
  matter for safety.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35500980

- **Biomechanical characteristics of yoga manoeuvres — PMC, 2021.** Reports
  joint-angle and range-of-motion data for poses including Warrior II and
  Triangle, the kind of reference ranges that inform objective posture
  assessment.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8378959

## Datasets for yoga computer vision

- **Yoga pose dataset for computer vision — PMC.** A labelled dataset of
  11,344 images across 10 asanas, 80 videos and 4 viewing angles, built for
  pose-recognition research.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10293982

- **Yoga dataset — Zenodo (open access).** The open-access companion release
  of the computer-vision yoga dataset above.
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7818789

## General health guidance

- **National guidance on yoga for wellbeing.** For general questions about
  whether yoga helps a condition, national health services publish balanced
  overviews — but these are general information, not personal medical advice.
  Anyone with an injury, pregnancy or a medical condition should consult a
  qualified teacher or doctor before starting or changing a practice.
